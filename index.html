Privacy Policy for Ripple

Introduction

Ripple is owned and operated by Gigi Digital LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

Ripple is a platform designed to encourage small, real-world actions that improve local communities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using Ripple, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We may collect limited information when you create or use an account, including:

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Optional profile information
  • Messages, feedback, or support requests you send to us

Automatically Collected Information

When you use Ripple, certain technical information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Device type and operating system
  • Browser type
  • App usage data
  • General location information (if permission is granted)

This information helps us operate and improve the Ripple platform.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the Ripple service
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Show relevant community actions based on location
  • Monitor misuse, abuse, or security issues
  • Respond to support requests and user feedback

Ripple does not sell personal information to third parties.

Location Information

Ripple may request access to general location information to show community actions near you.

Location sharing is optional and can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

Data Sharing

We may share limited information only in the following situations:

  • With trusted service providers that help operate the platform (such as hosting or analytics providers)
  • If required by law, regulation, or legal request
  • To protect the safety, rights, or security of users, Ripple, or the public

We do not sell or rent personal information.

Data Security

Gigi Digital LLC takes reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

However, no online system can be completely secure.

Children's Privacy

Ripple is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If such information is discovered, we will delete it promptly.

Your Choices and Rights

Users may:

  • Update or correct their account information
  • Disable location permissions in device settings
  • Request deletion of their account and personal data

To request deletion, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Continued use of Ripple after updates means you accept the revised policy.
